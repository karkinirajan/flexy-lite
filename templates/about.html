About FlexyLite

Inventory control with less noise and more signal.

FlexyLite is a focused inventory workspace for small grocery operations. It keeps product data, stock levels, and location details visible without crowding the screen or the workflow.

The updated interface uses a responsive design, clearer hierarchy, and stronger visual contrast so daily stock work stays quick on desktop, tablet, and phone.

What it does

Tracks product SKU, quantity, warehouse location, and the latest action in one surface.

Why it is cleaner

Shared layout, accessible controls, and no legacy Bootstrap 3 dependencies.

Deployment ready

Environment-driven settings, Whitenoise static handling, and a uv-managed dependency manifest.

Responsive tables

Product rows stay readable on smaller screens without losing the editing flow.

Modern auth pages

Login, register, and password reset screens now match the main inventory experience.

Safer operations

Write actions are restricted to superusers and delete actions are POST-based.
